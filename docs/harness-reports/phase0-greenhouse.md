1. **Accuracy: no.** Plans list 0–4 fields of 16–25. The agent loops on comboboxes
   (re-clicks Country until the 30-step cap) and never writes the field list. One plan
   (discord) never finished: NIM 170 s timeouts.
2. **Cost: too high.** ~8 min and ~400k tokens for a plan that is 93% empty. NIM also
   returned 503 "overloaded" during the run.
3. **Model swaps didn't help** (figma trials): deepseek-v4.1-flash timed out on every call;
   nemotron-3-ultra ran 1649 s, 5 LLM calls, 0 fields.

**Recommendation:** ADR-016's "browser-use plans first" is not supported by the data. Keep
the extension's DOM-snapshot filler as the primary path and spend effort on its gaps
(robinhood: 0/25 filled, `ok=False`; free-text "Why X?" and yes/no screening questions go
to Review). Revisit a browser-use planner only with a DOM-extraction step (read fields from
the snapshot, not by clicking) or a faster/stronger model — neither is a small change.
