ApplyScout brand, round 3

One personality, one signature shape, and strict rules for when colour appears. Built on option 1 (ink and marigold, IBM Plex Sans).

Personality

A calm, honest coach. It speaks plainly, shows its work, and never hypes your chances.

Job hunting is anxious and full of tools that promise too much. ApplyScout earns trust the opposite way: it says what it did, what it will not do, and where it needs you. Every visual choice follows from that: quiet surfaces, one warm signal, real proof instead of decoration.

Voice Maggie writes in the first person, in short plain sentences, with numbers instead of adjectives.
WriteI stopped because the form asks if you can work in Singapore. I won’t guess this.
NotOops! Something went wrong. Please try again later.
Write7 sent today. 1 isn’t confirmed yet, so check your email from Kite Labs.
NotYou’re crushing it! 7 applications blasted out today!
WriteI didn’t add Kubernetes. You haven’t confirmed it in your facts.
NotWe optimised your resume with high-impact ATS keywords.
The signature: the marigold dot It comes from the logo and means one thing everywhere: this needs you. It replaces the yellow highlighter.

In the logo, the dot is the scout’s point: where attention goes.

2 need your answer

Before any heading or row that is waiting on the person. Never decorative.

Review 5

As the count on Review and on the phone tab: the only coloured badge in the product.

As the current step in a sequence (onboarding, Maggie’s progress on a form): done is ink, waiting on you is marigold.

Marigold is never text. On marigold, text is ink (10.3:1).

Imagery

  • Show the product telling the truth: real resume lines, the facts behind them, Maggie’s notes.
  • Explanations use simple line diagrams: one 1.5px ink stroke, marigold only for the step that needs you.
  • No stock photos of smiling people at laptops, no 3D shapes, no gradients, no AI-generated people.
  • No fake logos of employers or invented success numbers.
