Why it could winIt is the most credible for working professionals: no claims, just the machine doing the job in front of you. It also demos the truth check, which is the reason to trust ApplyScout over a keyword stuffer.
RiskDark, product-led sites are a common SaaS look (Linear, Raycast), so it is less ownable than the red pen. The animation must be fast and skippable, and the page must read fully without it.
What it needsReal motion design: about 3 seconds, one orchestrated moment, respecting reduced motion. “Try it on a job” would later need a real sandbox; until then it opens a recorded example.
