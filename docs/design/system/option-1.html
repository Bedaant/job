badge

Surfaces are separated by 1px borders. One shadow exists, for things that float (toasts, menus, dialogs).

Why, and the risk
TypeIBM Plex Sans has matching Devanagari, Arabic and Thai families, so Hindi, Arabic and Thai screens later keep one voice.
ColourBlack buttons read as serious and calm, and colour is spent only on what needs attention. Marigold is warm and positive in India and belongs to no competitor here.
Shape6px controls and 10px surfaces: crisp without feeling sharp. Borders separate surfaces; only floating things get a shadow.
RiskBlack and warm grey can feel stark if the copy is cold. Marigold can never be text (2:1); it is only a fill under dark text.
