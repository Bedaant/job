How it works

  1. Step 1Confirm your factsYour resume becomes 25 to 40 short facts. Fix anything that’s wrong.
  2. Step 2Maggie finds and tailorsShe matches roles to your whole profile and rewrites your resume for each.
  3. Step 3You approve, she appliesPress Send yourself, or let her send within your daily limit.

What Maggie will never do

  • Invent experience
    Every line traces back to a fact you confirmed.
  • Type your password
    At a sign-in page she stops and hands the form to you.
  • Guess your work rights or demographics
    Those answers come from you, or your saved answers.
  • Send more than you allow
    A daily limit you set, and a pause that works at once.
